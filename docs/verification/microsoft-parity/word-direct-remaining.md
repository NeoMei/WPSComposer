# Remaining Mac Word direct method surface

This static inventory follows the frozen 80 Word direct-method rows. After the two business-method slices, 35 names are declared by MacWordSession and 45 remain absent. Declaration is not native verification or complete argument support. Equivalent behavior exercised through the generation pipeline does not certify the same direct session method. Shared lifecycle, property, format, target and verb rows remain separate.
